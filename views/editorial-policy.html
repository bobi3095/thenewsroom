About Us

Editorial Policy

Our Mission

The News Room is committed to independent, honest and fearless journalism. We believe the public deserves accurate, fair and complete information — especially the stories that mainstream media chooses to ignore. Our mission is to cover the truth, wherever it leads.

Independence

The News Room operates independently. We are not affiliated with any political party, corporate group or government body. Our editorial decisions are made solely on the basis of news value and public interest. No advertiser, sponsor or external party has any influence over our reporting.

Accuracy & Verification

We are committed to publishing only verified information. Before publishing, our team verifies facts through multiple reliable sources. If an error is made, we correct it promptly and transparently, with a clear correction notice on the original article.

Fairness & Balance

We strive to present all sides of a story fairly. When covering controversial issues, we make every effort to include diverse perspectives. We clearly distinguish between news reporting and opinion/editorial content.

Sources

We protect the identity of confidential sources when necessary for their safety. We are transparent about the sources we use and clearly attribute information to its origin. Anonymous sources are used only when there is no other way to obtain critical information in the public interest.

Corrections Policy

We take errors seriously. If you believe something we have published is factually incorrect, please contact us at thenewsroom.tnr.in@gmail.com. We will investigate promptly and publish a correction if warranted.

Advertising

Advertising revenue helps sustain our journalism. However, advertising has absolutely no influence on our editorial content. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labeled as such and kept separate from our news reporting.

Contact

For editorial queries, corrections or feedback, reach us at thenewsroom.tnr.in@gmail.com
